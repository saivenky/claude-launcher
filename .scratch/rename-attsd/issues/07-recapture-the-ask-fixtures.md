# 07 — Re-capture the ask fixtures on the new socket

**What to build:** the `ask_single` and `ask_toggled` fixtures become true
recordings again. Each has a `.jsonl`, a `.pane`, and an `.ansi`, and all three
are regenerated by running the capture against socket `attsd` — not by
search-and-replacing the old ones.

The distinction matters. `.pane` and `.ansi` are hard-wrapped terminal frames;
`claude-launcher` appears inside output that a real terminal broke at a real
column. Substituting a 5-character name leaves the break where the
15-character one put it, producing a "terminal frame" no terminal could have
emitted — and the `.ansi` carries escape sequences positioned against those
same columns.

Re-capturing also proves the drained-and-resumed socket works, which is the one
thing the cutover cannot verify from the inside.
